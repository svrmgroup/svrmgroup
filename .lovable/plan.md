# Cape Town Chauffeur Search Ads — Enquiries

A small Google Search campaign that shows text ads when people search for a chauffeur, private driver or airport pickup in Cape Town. It sends them to your matching page, and success is counted as a WhatsApp tap or an enquiry form sent. Nothing spends money until you approve the daily budget and the launch on an approval card.

## What the research found
Search volumes are Semrush estimates for South African searches, checked 21 Oct 2026.
- **"chauffeur service Cape Town":** about 260 searches a month, around $0.67 a click, medium ad competition.
- **"private driver Cape Town":** about 110 a month, high ad competition.
- **"Cape Town airport transfer":** about 170 a month, around $1.46 a click, the priciest term.
- **"airport shuttle Cape Town":** about 480 a month, but this is budget shuttle intent, so it gets excluded rather than targeted.
- **"luxury chauffeur Cape Town" and "corporate chauffeur Cape Town":** too few searches to measure. They stay in as low-cost extras.
- **Competitors:** your saved research shows Cape Town Concierge and V-Class for Hire publish lower V-Class day rates (R7,000–R9,850). The ads will sell the luxury fleet and the Maybach, not the lowest price.
- **Not checked yet:** competitors' live Google ads. I'll check them in the first build step and save the findings to your marketing documents.

## Campaign shape
- **Type:** Google Search only.
- **Where the ads show:** the Cape Town area, plus the UK, US, UAE and Saudi Arabia for searches that mention Cape Town. This covers people planning a trip.
- **Language:** English.
- **Ad groups and landing pages:**
  1. Chauffeur service — the chauffeur page
  2. Airport transfers — the airport transfers page
  3. Corporate and executive — the chauffeur page
  4. Private tours with a driver — the tours page
- **Excluded searches:** cheap, shuttle, bus, Uber, Bolt, jobs and driver vacancies, so you don't pay for the wrong clicks.
- **Counted as a lead:** a WhatsApp tap (tracking is already installed) and an enquiry form sent (I'll add this). Page views never count as leads.

## Technical details
- Google Search campaign with Maximize Conversions bidding. The goal is the existing WhatsApp contact conversion plus a new enquiry-submit conversion fired only after a successful database insert.
- Use phrase and exact match. Each ad group links to its landing page with the right page-specific WhatsApp message.
- The brief is saved in your ads briefs folder and grouped in a Files collection. Competitor findings are saved with your marketing documents.
- Location targeting uses "presence or interest" for the international markets, so searches that mention Cape Town qualify.
